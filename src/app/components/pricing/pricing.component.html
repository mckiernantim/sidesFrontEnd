What's the difference between Basic and Pro?

Basic gives you session-based sides generation — upload a script, make your sides, done. Pro adds persistent projects, shoot-day scheduling, cast management, callsheet generation, and opt-in encrypted script storage so your work is always there when you return.

What does Team include?

Team gives up to 5 people on your production full Pro access — shared projects, schedules, and callsheet delivery to the whole crew. Contact us to get set up.

What is the Founders Rate?
