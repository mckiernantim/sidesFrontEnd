How AI Validation Works

In rare instances, some lines are ambiguously categorized during our scan. We partner with Anthropic to validate ambiguous lines for proper classification.

  • No data is saved. EVER.
  • Smart & Efficient: Only ambiguous lines are sent to AI (~200 lines max)
  • Higher Accuracy: Catches edge cases rule-based scanning might miss
