1. Introduction

SidesWays ("we," "us," or "our") operates the SidesWays web application at sides-ways.com (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have in connection with that information. By using the Service you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account data: When you sign in via Google OAuth we receive your name, email address, and profile photo URL from Google.
  • Script files: PDF files you upload for sides generation. See Section 4 for our strict data-handling policy on these files.
  • Contact messages: If you contact us via the contact form, we receive your name, email, and message content.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Log data: IP address, browser type, referring URLs, pages visited, and timestamps, collected automatically when you use the Service.
  • Usage data: Features used, actions taken, and session duration to help us improve the product.
  • Cookies: Session and preference cookies. See our Cookie Policy for details.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your identity and manage your account.
  • To process your uploaded script files and generate sides PDFs.
  • To respond to customer support inquiries.
  • To improve the reliability and features of the Service.
  • To send transactional emails (job completion notifications, account updates). We do not send marketing emails without your explicit consent.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. Script File Handling

We understand that production scripts are confidential. We take the following binding commitments regarding your uploaded files:

  • No permanent storage: Uploaded script PDFs are processed in memory and deleted from our servers within 10 minutes of job completion. Deleted scripts must be reuploaded to generate new sides
  • No reading or indexing: We parse your scripts only to extract the data necessary to generate sides. We do not read, store, or index script content for any other purpose.
  • No sharing: We will never share, sell, or transfer your script files to any third party.
  • Encryption in transit: All file transfers occur over TLS 1.2 or higher.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services which may process your data under their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase / Google Cloud: Authentication, hosting, and database. Governed by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Stripe: Payment processing. We do not store payment card data. Governed by the Stripe Privacy Policy.

6. Data Retention

Account data is retained for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by emailing tim@sides-ways.com. Script files are deleted automatically within 24 hours of job completion regardless of account status.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at tim@sides-ways.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects when the most recent changes were made. Continued use of the Service after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
