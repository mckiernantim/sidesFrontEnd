1. Introduction

SidesWays ("we," "us," or "our") operates the SidesWays web application at sides-ways.com (the "Service").

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how long we keep it, and the choices available to you.

By using the Service, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account information: When you sign in through Google OAuth, we receive information such as your name, email address, and profile information made available by Google.
  • Script files: PDF files and other documents you upload for sides generation. These files are handled as described in Section 4.
  • Contact information: If you contact us through our contact form or by email, we may receive your name, email address, and the contents of your message.

We do not ask for or receive your payment card number. Card details are entered directly on Stripe's secure pages, as described in Section 5.

2.2 Billing and Subscription Information

If you subscribe to a paid plan, we store limited billing records associated with your account so we can determine your access to paid features and provide support. This includes:

  • A Stripe customer identifier linking your account to your Stripe billing record.
  • Your subscription status, plan name, price, and billing interval.
  • Your current billing period start and end dates, and whether a cancellation is scheduled.
  • Payment outcomes, such as whether the most recent payment succeeded or failed, the amount, and the date.
  • Discount eligibility flags, as described in Section 2.3.

We do not store full payment card numbers, card expiration dates, or card security codes on our servers.

2.3 Discount Eligibility Information

To administer discounted pricing, we may automatically evaluate information already associated with your account:

  • Student Rate: We check whether the email address on your account ends in .edu. If it does, we create a record noting your email address, the date the Student Rate was granted, and the date the two-year eligibility window expires.
  • Founders Rate: We check whether your account appears on a fixed internal list of eligible early users.

These checks are automated and are used only to determine which price applies to you. When a Student Rate window expires, we retain a record that eligibility was granted and has ended, so that the discount is not issued twice for the same account. The applicable terms of these rates are described in our Terms of Service.

2.4 Information Collected Automatically

  • Log information: We may automatically collect information such as your IP address, browser type, referring URLs, pages visited, and timestamps associated with your use of the Service.
  • Usage information: We may collect information about features used, actions taken within the Service, and session activity to maintain, secure, and improve the Service. This includes a count of documents you have generated and the date of your most recent generation, which we use to operate your account and support the Service.
  • Cookies and browser storage: We use cookies and browser storage as described in our Cookie Policy.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use information we collect to:

  • Authenticate you and manage your account.
  • Process uploaded files and generate sides documents.
  • Provide, maintain, secure, and troubleshoot the Service.
  • Process payments, administer subscriptions and discount eligibility, and determine your access to paid features.
  • Respond to customer support requests.
  • Improve the reliability, functionality, and performance of the Service.
  • Send transactional communications, such as job completion notifications and important account or service updates.
  • Detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, abuse, security incidents, and violations of our Terms.
  • Comply with applicable laws and legal obligations.

We do not use your uploaded scripts or generated sides to send you marketing communications without your consent.

4. Script File Handling

We understand that production scripts may contain confidential and commercially sensitive material. We design SidesWays to minimize the amount of time these files remain in our systems.

Temporary Processing

Uploaded script files are processed for the purpose of generating sides documents. Subject to the limitations described below, we do not retain uploaded script files indefinitely.

Uploaded script files are automatically deleted from our primary application storage within 10 minutes of job completion.

Generated Sides PDFs

Generated sides PDFs are made available for download for a limited period, typically up to 10 minutes. After that period, they are automatically deleted from our primary application storage.

Limited Purpose

We process uploaded documents only as necessary to provide the Service, including extracting information needed to generate sides documents.

We do not use uploaded scripts or generated sides for unrelated purposes.

No AI Training

We do not use uploaded scripts, generated sides, or other document content submitted to SidesWays to train artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

Access

Access to uploaded documents and generated sides is limited to the systems and personnel who require access to operate, maintain, secure, or support the Service.

No Sale

We do not sell uploaded scripts, generated sides, or your personal information.

We do not share document content with third parties except where necessary to provide the Service, comply with applicable law, protect the security of the Service, or otherwise as described in this Privacy Policy.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information transmitted to and stored by the Service.

File transfers between your browser and the Service are encrypted using TLS.

No method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Third-Party Services and Service Providers

We use third-party service providers to operate portions of the Service. Depending on how you use SidesWays, these providers may process information on our behalf.

These services may include:

  • Google / Firebase: Authentication, hosting, databases, and related infrastructure. Governed by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Document-processing infrastructure: Certain document scanning, classification, processing, or PDF-generation services may temporarily receive uploaded scripts or related session information solely to provide the Service. Any such processing is subject to our contractual and operational requirements for handling user data.
  • Stripe: Payment processing. Stripe processes payment card information on our behalf. We do not store full payment card numbers on our servers. Governed by the Stripe Privacy Policy.

Third-party providers may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable contractual obligations.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain account information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, maintain your account, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce our agreements, and protect the Service.

Uploaded scripts and generated sides PDFs are retained only for the temporary processing and download periods described in Section 4, subject to limited retention that may occur in backups, logs, security systems, or where required by law.

Billing and subscription records described in Section 2.2 are retained for as long as reasonably necessary to administer your subscription and to meet our accounting, tax, audit, and legal recordkeeping obligations, which may extend beyond the closure of your account. Stripe retains its own records in accordance with its policies and legal obligations.

You may delete documents through the Service where that functionality is available. Deleting a document also removes associated document-session information maintained by the Service.

You may request deletion of your account and associated personal information by contacting us at admin@sides-ways.com.

We will process deletion requests in accordance with applicable law and our legitimate operational and legal requirements.
