1. Cookies and Browser Storage

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Websites can also use related browser technologies, such as localStorage and IndexedDB, which store information in your browser but are not automatically sent along with network requests.

This policy covers both. We use the term "browser storage" to refer to these technologies collectively.

2. What We Use

2.1 Cookies Set by SidesWays

SidesWays does not set any first-party cookies. We do not use cookies to authenticate you, to track you, or to remember your preferences.

Instead, the information the Service needs is kept in your browser's local storage, as described in Sections 2.2 and 2.3. Cookies may still be set by the third-party providers described in Section 2.5.

2.2 Strictly Necessary Browser Storage

This storage is required for the Service to function. Without it, features like signing in and staying signed in will not work. It cannot be disabled while you are using the Service.

Stored By Purpose Duration
Firebase Authentication (Google) Keeps you signed in between visits so you do not have to re-authenticate each time. Stored in your browser's local storage and IndexedDB by the Firebase SDK. Until you sign out or clear site data

2.3 Functional Browser Storage

This storage remembers a display preference so the interface looks the way you left it.

Data Purpose Duration
Theme preference Remembers whether you selected the light or dark interface theme Until you clear site data

Temporary storage used while generating a document is described separately in Section 2.4.

2.4 Temporary Document-Generation Storage

We use your browser's localStorage to temporarily maintain information needed during document generation and your current session. localStorage is stored in your browser and is not automatically included with network requests. SidesWays may access specific values when needed to provide features such as document processing and download authorization.

The following types of information may be stored temporarily:

Data Purpose Duration
Download authorization token Authorizes access to your generated PDF Up to 10 minutes
Token expiration time Displays the download expiration countdown Up to 10 minutes
Uploaded filename Labels your generated document and maintains session continuity Session
Call sheet information Maintains call sheet information associated with your current document Session
Selected scenes and scene order Maintains your selections during document generation Session
Processing status Tracks your active document-generation session Until processing completes

None of this browser storage is intended to persist beyond your active session. Temporary document-related data is cleared when you return to the home page, complete a download, or delete your document.

2.5 Third-Party Cookies

Google Sign-In: When you authenticate via Google OAuth, Google may set cookies governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Stripe: Checkout and subscription management take place on Stripe-hosted pages. Stripe may set cookies and use browser storage on those pages, including for fraud prevention and payment security. This activity is governed by the Stripe Privacy Policy.

These providers set this storage on their own domains and under their own policies. We do not control it, and it is not covered by Section 2.1.

2.6 Analytics and Advertising

We do not use analytics cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party tracking technologies on the Service.

If we introduce analytics in the future, we will update this policy before doing so and will obtain consent where required by applicable law.

3. Managing Cookies & Storage

You can clear browser storage for this site, including localStorage and IndexedDB, through your browser's privacy or site-settings controls. Clearing it will sign you out and discard any in-progress document session. Temporary document-generation items are also cleared automatically as described in Section 2.4.

You can control third-party cookies, such as those set by Google or Stripe, through your browser settings. Blocking storage required for authentication will prevent you from signing in to the Service.
