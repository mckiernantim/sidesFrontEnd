1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They allow the site to remember your actions and preferences (such as login status) over a period of time, so you don't have to re-enter them each time you return.

2. Cookies We Use

2.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are required for the Service to function. Without them, features like authentication and session management will not work. They cannot be disabled.

Cookie Name Purpose Duration
__session Firebase authentication session token Session
sw_auth_state Stores authentication state to prevent unnecessary re-authentication 7 days

2.2 Functional Cookies

These cookies enhance your experience by remembering your preferences.

Cookie Name Purpose Duration
sw_ui_prefs Stores user interface preferences (e.g., last selected call sheet settings) 30 days

2.3 Analytics Cookies

We currently do not use third-party analytics cookies. If we add analytics in the future, this policy will be updated and you will be notified before any analytics cookies are set.

2.4 Local Browser Storage (localStorage)

In addition to cookies, we use your browser's localStorage to store temporary session data during document generation. localStorage is never automatically sent to our servers — it is only accessible by the SidesWays application running in your browser. The following items are stored:

Key Purpose Duration
pdfBackupToken Temporary signed token that authorizes your PDF download on the /complete page. Never shared or transmitted except as an HTTP header when you click Download. Cleared when you download, delete your document, or navigate away
pdfTokenExpires Expiration timestamp for the PDF token. Used to drive the on-screen countdown and auto-delete the document after 10 minutes. Cleared with pdfBackupToken
name Your uploaded script filename, used to label your download and maintain session continuity across the upload → dashboard → complete flow. Cleared at end of session
callsheetData / callSheetPath Temporary call sheet data attached to your current document session. Cleared at end of session
selectedScenes / sceneOrder The scenes you have selected and their order within your current session. Cleared at end of session
currentJobId Tracks the active processing job so the app can poll for completion status. Cleared when the job completes

None of this data persists beyond your active session. All localStorage items related to document state are cleared when you return to the home page, complete a download, or delete your document.

2.5 Third-Party Cookies

Google Sign-In: When you authenticate via Google OAuth, Google may set cookies governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Stripe: If you complete a payment, Stripe may set cookies governed by the Stripe Privacy Policy.

3. Managing Cookies & Storage

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Note that blocking strictly necessary cookies will prevent you from logging in to the Service. To clear localStorage data, open your browser's developer tools → Application → Local Storage → sides-ways.com and delete the keys listed in Section 2.4. All localStorage items are also cleared automatically when your session ends.
